# 💬 Examples for the Prompts block

---

## Why is this file needed?

This file puts examples for the Prompts block into a separate material so that the speaker can:
- do not overload the main theoretical file;
- quickly select 1-2 demonstrations for the audience;
- show how a high-quality prompt is born from a raw request.

---

## Example 1. Draft letter to client

### Situation
You need to quickly inform the customer about a delivery delay.

### Basic prompt
```text
Write a letter to the client about the delay of the order.
```

### Improved prompt
```text
You are a customer support specialist.
Write a short, polite letter to the client about the order being delayed by 2 days.
Structure:
1. Apology
2. Reason without technical details
3. New delivery date
4. Compensation in the form of a 10% discount
Tone: businesslike, friendly.
Language: Russian.
```

### What to showaudience
- How the quality of the answer changes after adding structure.
- That prompt in itself does not guarantee a unified corporate style.
- Why Instructions will be needed later for mass use.

### Conclusion
Prompt is good for a quick start when you need to get a draft in 1-2 minutes.

## Example 2. Quick brief on the topic

### Situation
The employee needs to quickly understand an unfamiliar topic before a meeting.

### Prompt
```text
Explain the topic "Model Context Protocol" to a novice developer.
Format:
1. 5 theses
2. 1 simple usage example
3. 3 common mistakes
```

### What to show the audience
- Prompt can dramatically reduce the time it takes to enter a topic.
-It is particularly useful as an intellectual rough-in rather than as a definitive source of truth.
- For questions with a high cost of error, you needo Check facts and sources.

### Conclusion
Prompts work great as a quick layer of research and preparation.

---

## How to use on slides

- In the short version of the speech: take just one example.
- In the full version: show two prompts and compare the quality of the answer.
- In live-demo: first enter a short prompt, then immediately strengthen it with structure.
