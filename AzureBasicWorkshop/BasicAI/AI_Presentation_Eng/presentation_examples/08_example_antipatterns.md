# ⚠️ Examples of antipatterns

---

## Why is this file needed?

This file displays typical architecture selection errors in a separate block so that they can be shown as independent slides or used in Q&A.

---

## Antipattern 1. "Agent for everything"

### Problem
Increasing costs, unpredictable behavior and monitoring difficulties.

### Countermeasure
Use the agent only in complex adaptive nodes, and design the rest as a workflow.

---

## Antipattern 2. Prompt-only in production

### Problem
There is no repeatability, uniform rules and quality control.

### Countermeasure
Add instructions, skills and measurable quality criteria.

---

## Antipattern 3. Over-privileged MCP

### Problem
Risk of security incidents and uncontrolled uncontrolled actions.

### Countermeasure
Least privilege principle, audit challengeov and approval for sensitive operations.

---

## How to use at a performance

- In the short format, take one antipattern.
- In a long format, show all three as a block of engineering thinking.
- Audience question: “Where do you already have one of these risks in your current projects?”
