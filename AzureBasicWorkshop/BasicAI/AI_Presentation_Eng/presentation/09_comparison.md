# Comparison Matrix

## Full Comparison

| Criterion | Prompts | Instructions | Skills | MCPs | Agents | Workflows |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Setup complexity | Low | Low | Medium | Medium | High | Very High |
| Autonomy | None | None | Low | None | High | Process-driven |
| Flexibility | High | Medium | Medium | Medium | High | Medium/Low |
| Predictability | Low | Medium | Medium | High (tool layer) | Low/Medium | High |
| Typical cost | Low | Low | Low/Med | Medium | High | Medium |
| Needed expertise | Basic | Basic | Intermediate | Intermediate | Advanced | Advanced |
| Best for | One-offs | Consistency | Reuse | Integrations | Exploration | Operations |

---

## Strength/Weakness Snapshot

- Prompts: speed and simplicity, weak governance.
- Instructions: consistency, still no live data.
- Skills: reusable quality, maintenance needed.
- MCPs: real-world access, requires security design.
- Agents: adaptive autonomy, higher risk and cost.
- Workflows: reliability and auditability, less flexibility.

---

## "Who Wins When" Matrix

| Scenario | Best Choice |
|---|---|
| One quick answer | Prompt |
| Team assistant behavior control | Instructions |
| Repeatable analysis pattern | Skill |
| Need DB/API/file access | MCP |
| Open-ended investigation | Agent |
| Regulated recurring process | Workflow |

---

## Combined Patterns

1. Product assistant: Instructions + Skills + MCPs.
2. Research copilot: Instructions + MCPs + Agent.
3. Enterprise automation: Workflow + Skills + MCPs (+ optional Agent node).

---

## Speaker Notes

- Use this as the decision anchor.
- Explain trade-offs, not winners.
