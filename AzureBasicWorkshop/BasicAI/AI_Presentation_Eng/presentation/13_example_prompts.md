# 💬 Examples for the Prompts Block

---

## Why This File Is Needed

This file puts examples for the Prompts block into a separate material so that the speaker can:
- avoid overloading the main theoretical file;
- quickly select 1-2 demonstrations for the audience;
- show how a high-quality prompt is born from a raw request.

---

## Example 1. Draft Letter to a Customer

### Situation
You need to quickly inform the customer about a delivery delay.

### Basic prompt
```text
Write a letter to the customer about the order delay.
```

### Improved prompt
```text
You are a customer support specialist.
Write a short polite letter to the customer about a 2-day order delay.
Structure:
1. Apology
2. Reason without technical details
3. New delivery date
4. Compensation in the form of a 10% discount
Tone: formal, friendly.
Language: English.
```

### What to show the audience
- How the quality of the answer changes after adding structure.
- That a prompt on its own does not guarantee a unified corporate style.
- Why Instructions will be needed later for team-wide use.

### Conclusion
A prompt is good for a quick start when you need a draft in 1-2 minutes.

## Example 2. Quick Brief on a Topic

### Situation
An employee needs to quickly understand an unfamiliar topic before a meeting.

### Prompt
```text
Explain the topic "Model Context Protocol" for a beginner developer.
Format:
1. 5 key points
2. 1 simple usage example
3. 3 common mistakes
```

### What to show the audience
- A prompt can dramatically reduce the time needed to get up to speed on a topic.
- It is particularly useful as an intellectual rough draft rather than a definitive source of truth.
- For questions with a high cost of error, always verify facts and sources.

### Conclusion
Prompts work great as a quick research and preparation layer.

## How to Use on Slides

- In a short talk: use just one example.
- In the full version: show two prompts side by side and compare answer quality.
- In live-demo: first enter a short prompt, then immediately strengthen it with structure.
